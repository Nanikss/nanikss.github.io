Roju Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Roju ("day" in Telugu) is a daily check-in service. Once a day it sends an elderly parent a short WhatsApp message asking how they are, and tells their family member (the "caregiver") the answer. This page explains what personal data Roju handles, why, and how you can have it deleted. Roju is currently a small pilot run by Kumar Nenavath in Hyderabad, India.

Who uses Roju

  • Caregivers sign in to the Roju Android app with their WhatsApp number and add a parent.
  • Parents do not install anything. They receive WhatsApp messages and tap a button, type a reply, or give a missed call.
  • Local contacts (optional) are relatives or neighbours who receive alerts on WhatsApp.

Consent comes first

Before any daily check-in starts, the parent receives a WhatsApp message explaining the service and must tap to agree. If they decline, nothing is sent. A parent can stop at any time by sending STOP on WhatsApp, and restart by sending START.

What we collect

  • Caregiver: name and WhatsApp number.
  • Parent: name, what the caregiver calls them (e.g. "Amma"), WhatsApp number, preferred language, daily check-in time, and the list of medicines the caregiver chooses to enter.
  • Local contact: name, WhatsApp number and language.
  • Check-in records: the parent's answers (for example "fine", "not feeling well", medicines taken), the time of each reply, missed calls to the Roju number, and any text the parent sends.
  • Message records: a log of the messages Roju sends, so we can fix problems.

We do not collect location, contacts lists, photos from your phone, or payment details. Roju gives no medical advice and is not an emergency service; in an emergency, call 108.

How we use it

  • To send the daily check-in, reminders and medicine reminders to the parent.
  • To tell the caregiver (and local contact, for alerts) how the parent is, including immediate alerts when the parent is unwell, asks for a call, does not reply, or mentions something that looks like a scam.
  • To show the caregiver each day's status and recent history in the app.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers needed to run Roju.

Service providers

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) delivers the messages, under the WhatsApp Business terms.
  • Hosting and telephony providers run the Roju server and the missed-call number.

How long we keep it

Check-in records and message logs are kept while the service is active so the family can see recent history, and old operational logs are cleared regularly. When a caregiver removes a parent in the app, that parent's profile, check-ins and related records are deleted.

Your rights and deleting your data

  • Parents can send STOP at any time to end check-ins, and can ask for their data to be deleted.
  • Caregivers can remove a parent in the app, which deletes that parent's data.
  • Anyone can ask to see, correct or delete their data by emailing the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Login codes and session tokens are stored only in hashed form, and access to each parent's data is limited to the caregiver who added them.

Children

Roju is meant for adults. Caregivers must be 18 or older.

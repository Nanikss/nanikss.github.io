Roju

రోజు · a daily check-in for parents who live alone

Every day at a time the family chooses, Roju sends a parent one short WhatsApp message in Telugu, Hindi or English, asking how they are. The parent taps a button, and their son or daughter gets the answer, wherever they live.

How it works

  1. A family member adds their parent in the Roju app.
  2. The parent gets a WhatsApp message asking for their consent. Nothing starts until they agree.
  3. Each day the parent taps "I'm fine", "Not feeling well" or "Please call me". They can also give a missed call.
  4. The family gets a daily summary, and an immediate alert if the parent is unwell, asks for a call, or doesn't reply.

Not an emergency service. Roju gives no medical advice. In an emergency, call 108.

Status

Roju is a small pilot based in Hyderabad, India.
